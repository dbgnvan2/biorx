Out-of-scope finding (does not affect the verdict)
==================================================
The first full-suite run of this gate failed once on
tests/test_h_environment.py::test_h_pdf_download_sends_biorx_user_agent
(asserts a bare `biorx/1.0` User-Agent). Two immediate re-runs were green
(1596 passed). Root cause is pre-existing and unrelated to D1/D2: that test is
a leftover from the T2.3 change (a92857b, already in origin/main) — it was not
updated to pin BIORX_CONTACT_EMAIL to empty, unlike its sibling
test_t23_unpaywall_and_pdf_download_carry_the_contact_address. When the real
.env (BIORX_CONTACT_EMAIL=davegalloway@me.com) leaks into os.environ during a
run, polite_user_agent appends the mailto and the bare-`==` assertion fails.
It passes in CI (BIORX_CONTACT_EMAIL="") and in isolation. Recommended follow-up
(separate change): pin BIORX_CONTACT_EMAIL to "" in that test, or fold it into
test_t23.
